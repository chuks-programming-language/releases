# Contributing to Chuks

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Chuks. The compiler repository is
currently private and invitation-only. This isn't because we don't value community
involvement. It's because compiler correctness is the foundation of everything, and
changes there need contributors who already have deep familiarity with the specific
architecture.

The best way to show that commitment is to:

1. **Build packages in pure Chuks** and publish them to the [registry](https://registry.chuks.org).
2. **File detailed bug reports** with reproduction cases.
3. **Contribute to the standard library** (coming soon as a semi-open repo).
4. **Engage in the community** and show a deep understanding of how Chuks works.

We're watching, and we do invite people based on demonstrated commitment. The compiler
team is small by design, but the ecosystem is completely open to everyone.

## How to start today

### Build and publish packages

The fastest way to make an impact is to build something useful in pure Chuks and ship
it. See the [package management guide](https://chuks.org/guide/package-management/) to
get started, then publish to the [Chuks Package Registry](https://registry.chuks.org).

### Report bugs

Good bug reports are genuinely valuable, and a strong track record of them is one of the
clearest signals we look for. Please use the issue templates:

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/chuks-programming-language/releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/chuks-programming-language/releases/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
- [Share feedback](https://github.com/chuks-programming-language/releases/issues/new?template=feedback.yml)

A great bug report includes the Chuks version (`chuks --version`), your OS, a minimal
`.chuks` file that reproduces the issue, what you expected, and what actually happened.

### Join the community

Connect with other developers, ask questions, and show how you work:
[Chuks community](https://cleset.com/community/ChuksProgrammingLanguage?t=567d480d-7a28-4782-b8d0-db097adc2010).
